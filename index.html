Head coach · Founder

Jerry Luhukay

Jeremias Abraham Luhukay

A prominent Indonesian Muay Thai coach, fight promoter, and entrepreneur — and a foundational figure in popularizing and professionalizing Muay Thai in Indonesia. After studying and mastering the art in Thailand, Jerry returned home and founded Zealot Muay Thai in 2011/2012. What began as a local community grew into a major training camp and talent incubator, with hubs including Jakarta and Bali.

  • Founder of Zealot Muay Thai Built from Thailand training into a multi-branch Indonesian camp and talent pathway.
  • Promoter of Road to Victory (RTV) Lead promoter of the RTV fight series — a formal platform for amateur, semi-pro, and pro fighters across Indonesia.
  • PBMI Pro Division leadership Appointed to lead the Professional Division under PBMI, driving infrastructure, commercialization, and athletic growth of pro Muay Thai in Indonesia.

One hour with Jerry. Years of experience behind every round.
